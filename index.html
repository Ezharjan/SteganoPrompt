Hide a tripwire instruction inside any text

Paste an assignment prompt, choose a hidden instruction, and SteganoPrompt embeds it using Unicode Tag characters (U+E0000–U+E007F) — invisible to humans, but tokenized and read by most LLMs. If a student copies your text verbatim into ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, the model will follow the hidden cue and leave a tell-tale signature in the response.
